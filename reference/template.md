# Tips for Journal Club Presentations (residency template)

Keep it concise (10 minute presentation, leave about 10 min for discussion).

- **Start with**
  - Title of the paper
  - Lead author and institution
  - Journal it is published in
- **Introduction/background**: why the authors saw the need to do this study (2-3 sentences)
- **Objective(s)**
- **Method**: this part tells us the quality of the paper and whether the results are valid, so spend more time here
  - Study design (analytical vs descriptive, experimental vs observational; see the study design chart below)
  - Level of evidence
  - What, when, where, and how they formed the study population; how they built the study and control groups
  - How they defined outcome measures or other factors studied (e.g., if the outcome is UTI, was it based on culture results such as 10^5 colony count?)
  - Inclusion criteria (how representative is the sample?)
  - Exclusion criteria (possible confounders excluded, but did they overdo it?)
  - Data collection
  - Power of the study
- **Results**: list the essential results without speculation
- **Conclusion**: sum up the results in one sentence
- **Shortcomings**
  - Inclusion criteria (how representative is the sample?)
  - Exclusion criteria (did they overdo it?)
  - Control group appropriate? (apples vs oranges?)
  - Data collection (retrospective, prospective, from a large database, etc.)
  - Bias (selection, recall, etc.)
  - Power of the study (was there any type II error?)
  - Other drawbacks
- **Final comments, future perspectives**: would you change your practice based on this paper?

## Study design chart

- **Descriptive**: case report, case series, cross-sectional
- **Analytical**
  - **Observational**: case-control, cohort (retrospective or prospective)
  - **Experimental**: clinical randomized trials (CRT), lab research, animal research
